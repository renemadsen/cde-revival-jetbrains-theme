DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS
──────────────────────
• All corners: arc = 0 (sharp, rectangular)
• 3D bevel: Light top/left, dark bottom/right
• Button gradients: #AEB2C3 → #9CA0B1
• No transparency or blur effects
• Subdued, non-jarring colors
• Authentic CDE/Motif aesthetic

COMPARE TO CDE ORIGINAL

- CDE on Solaris 8/9/10
- CDE on AIX 5.x/6.x
- Motif 2.x applications
